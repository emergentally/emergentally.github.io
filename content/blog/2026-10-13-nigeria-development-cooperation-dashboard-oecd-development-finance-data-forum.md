Since 2024, Emergentally has been proud to work closely with Nigeria’s Federal Ministry of Budget and Economic Planning to develop the Nigeria Development Cooperation Dashboard. In recognition of Nigeria’s leadership on ensuring development resources are transparently managed, H.E. Dr. Doris Uzoka-Anite was invited to provide the keynote address to the OECD’s Development Finance Data Forum in Paris, on 8th October 2026.

## Recognition of the Dashboard as a practical instrument for managing development cooperation

The Dashboard is designed to integrate closely with countries’ own public financial management processes, rather than simply being an additional piece of software that countries have to maintain, manage and provide data to. H.E. Dr. Doris Uzoka-Anite said:

> “The Nigeria Development Cooperation Dashboard is evolving beyond a conventional database. It is becoming a practical instrument for planning, coordination, transparency and accountability in development cooperation.”

## Interoperability with international standards, including IATI

As the first system in the world to import IATI data on an ongoing basis, the Dashboard ensures that capturing data on a wide range of development partners’ activities is sustainable. It keeps the burden of data collection low while capturing high-quality, granular and timely data from partners:

> “The Dashboard consolidates information on development cooperation activities and resources reported by development partners, principally through the International Aid Transparency Initiative. It enables Government to see who is providing resources, what is being financed, where resources are being deployed, the scale of financing involved, and the development objectives being supported.”

Over the last year, we have further enhanced the interoperability of the Dashboard by strengthening the IFMIS integration, providing for automatic syncing of results data captured in DHIS2, and developing more complete API documentation.

## More still to come

We are continuously improving and developing the Dashboard. Going forward, we will make it possible to capture even more granular data in the Dashboard, and further streamline integrations with other systems. At the core of our design principles, we always strive to provide additional functionality while avoiding additional complexity, and constantly seek ways to simplify the existing system.
